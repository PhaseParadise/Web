Website & App Privacy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the phaseparadise.app website and the PhaseParadise mobile app handle data.

The website is static, uses no analytics, has no forms, and does not use advertising or tracking cookies. The app works locally on your device and does not require an account.
Website: no analytics, no newsletter, no login, no embedded maps or videos.
Fonts: website fonts are hosted locally, not loaded from Google at runtime.
Language choice: the website stores only your DE/EN preference in your browser.
App data: your personal app entries stay on your device.

1. Controller

Responsible for this website and the PhaseParadise app:

Elias Rist
Email: phaseparadise@gmail.com

Postal address details are not published online because this website is operated by a private individual. Legal or privacy-related requests can be sent by email.

2. Website Hosting

The website is hosted with GitHub Pages, a static hosting service by GitHub, Inc. When a visitor opens a GitHub Pages website, GitHub may process technical data such as IP address, browser request data, date and time of access, and security logs. This is necessary to deliver and protect the website.

3. Website Cookies, Local Storage & Analytics

This website does not use analytics tools, advertising pixels, contact forms, newsletter forms, logins, comments, or embedded third-party media.

The language switch stores the selected language in your browser's local storage under phaseparadise-lang. This is used only to remember whether you prefer the German or English landing page. It is not used for tracking.

4. Fonts

The website uses Archivo and IBM Plex Mono. These font files are hosted locally with the website. The visitor's browser does not contact Google Fonts to load the website typography.

5. External Links

The website links to Apple App Store, Google Play, and email. If you open those links, the respective provider may process data under its own privacy terms.

6. App Privacy

PhaseParadise is designed with privacy in mind. Personal information you enter, such as cycle entries, mood logs, notes, reminders, preferences, and optional partner information, is stored locally on your device.

This information is not uploaded to PhaseParadise servers and is not accessible by the developer. PhaseParadise does not require an account and does not use a cloud backend for personal app entries.

7. Data Stored on Your Device

  • Cycle data
  • Mood entries
  • Notes
  • Energy levels
  • Reminder settings
  • App preferences
  • Optional partner profile photo

You may optionally add a profile photo for your partner from your photo library or camera. This photo is stored only on your device in a private app-internal folder and displayed on the partner page.

Photo library or camera access is requested only when you choose to add a photo. The photo is not saved to your system photo gallery, not uploaded to a cloud backend, and not synchronized by PhaseParadise.

On iOS, the local database and partner photo folder are excluded from iCloud and iTunes backups. On Android, PhaseParadise excludes app data from Android Auto Backup and device-to-device transfer.

8. Crash Reports & Diagnostics

To improve app stability and troubleshoot issues, PhaseParadise uses Sentry, a third-party crash reporting service.

Limited technical data may include:

  • Device model
  • Operating system version
  • App version
  • Crash logs and diagnostic information

Personal notes, mood entries, and cycle data are not intentionally transmitted through crash reporting.

9. How Information Is Used

  • To deliver and secure the static website
  • To remember the visitor's language preference
  • To provide the app's core functionality locally on the device
  • To improve app reliability, stability, and performance

10. Data Sharing

PhaseParadise does not sell personal data. Limited technical website hosting data may be processed by GitHub Pages. Limited technical app diagnostic data may be processed by Sentry solely for crash reporting and troubleshooting.

11. Data Retention

Website language preference data remains in your browser until you clear site data or choose another language. Personal app data remains on your device until you delete it manually or uninstall the app.

Hosting and crash diagnostic data are retained by the respective service providers according to their own operational and security requirements.

12. Your Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, rectify, erase, restrict, object to processing, and receive a copy of your personal data. You may also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

For privacy requests, contact: phaseparadise@gmail.com

13. AI Notice

PhaseParadise does not currently use AI to make medical decisions, diagnoses, or treatment recommendations.

If AI-supported features are added in the future, this page will be updated to explain what the feature does, what data it uses, and important limitations.

14. Medical Disclaimer

PhaseParadise is intended for wellness, self-reflection, and informational purposes only. It is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, treatment, or contraception guidance.

15. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Updates will be published on this page.
